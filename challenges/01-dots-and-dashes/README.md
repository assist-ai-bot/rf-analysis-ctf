# 01 · Dots and Dashes

**Points:** 50 · **Level:** warm-up

## The story

Long before people talked on radios, they sent messages by switching a transmitter on and off.
Someone on our channel still does this. They are holding a real walkie-talkie and tapping its
talk button to spell out a message.

## Your files

| File                            | What it is                                    |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| `keyed_carrier.sigmf-data/meta` | An 11-second recording, 25 000 samples/second |

## What you are told

- The radio signal is either on or off. Nothing else about it changes.
- The message is in Morse code.
- The message is a few ordinary English words.

## The flag

Write the words in capital letters and join them with underscores.

Example: if the message is `HELLO WORLD`, the flag is `RFCTF{HELLO_WORLD}`.
